The Process

How It Works

01
Contact Us

Call us at (830) 406-2361 or fill out the contact form to get information about the bond.

02
Come In To The Office Or Fill Out Paperwork Online

Come into the office to fill out the paperwork, or if you are not from the area, fill out the paperwork online.

03
Pay The Bond Fee

Go to bond payment and make a payment for the bond fee.

04
Wait For The Defendant To Be Released

The bond will be submitted after the bond fee is paid, and the process of release will begin.

05
Wait For A Court Date To Be Set

The court will set a court date at a later date, it could take months, up to a couple years to receive a court date.

06
Finish The Case

After court dates, the case will get dismissed or disposed and you will be done with the case.
