2. What we do

Z Bail Bonds provides bail bond services in Hays, Guadalupe, Comal, Wilson, and Kendall Counties, Texas. This website gives information about our services and lets you contact us, fill out paperwork, check in, and make payments. Submitting a form does not guarantee that a bond will be approved or posted. The bond agreement you sign controls the bond itself, and if it conflicts with these terms, the bond agreement controls.

Prices depend on the type and amount of the bond. Bonds can be posted at our offices or online, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, subject to jail operating hours. Release usually happens 30 minutes to 1 hour after we receive payment and co-signer information, but it can take longer depending on how the jail operates. We cannot promise a release time.

3. Defendant and co-signer responsibilities

  • The defendant must go to every court date and follow every condition of the bond, including any required check-ins.
  • A co-signer agrees to pay any court fees incurred if the defendant fails to appear in court.
  • If a defendant misses court, the bond can be forfeited and the people who signed for it can owe the full bond amount, plus the costs of locating and returning the defendant.
  • You must keep your contact information up to date by calling or emailing us if your address, phone number, or email changes.

4. Payments, fees, and refunds

  • Payment is due in full when the order is placed.
  • A $25.00 non-refundable application fee is included in every bond premium.
  • We accept debit cards, major credit cards, cash, cashier's checks, and money orders. A payment authorization form must be signed for credit card payments made over the phone.
  • Once the jail has accepted a bond, no refunds are available. If a bond is not accepted, or you decide not to bond out before it is posted, return the bond to our office with your receipt and we will refund your payment minus the $25.00 application fee.
  • Refunds go back in the form you paid with. Credit card refunds can take 3 to 5 business days to appear.
