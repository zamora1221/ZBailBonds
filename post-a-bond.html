Steps To Post A Bond

Post A Bond

Follow these steps to get the paperwork moving. Call anytime if you get stuck — an agent can walk you through any part of it.

01
Contact An Agent

Reach out to an agent or call (830) 406-2361 to get started.

02
Fill Out The Cosigner Form

The cosigner completes the Cosigner Form online.

03
Submit Your Driver's License

Upload a copy of the cosigner's driver's license on the Cosigner Forms page, or email it to zbailbonds@gmail.com.

04
Pay The Bond Fee

Make your payment on the Bond Payment page.

05
Call To Verify

Call your agent at (830) 406-2361 to confirm everything was received.

06
Defendant Fills Out Forms

The defendant completes the forms on the Defendant Forms page, including the Defendant Form and the Defendant Promissory Note.
